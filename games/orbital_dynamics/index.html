Instructions:

Adjust the ORBITAL PARAMETERS below (i, Ω, M₀) to get the satellite to pass as close as possible to the target. To adjust the difficulty level, turn the different VIEWS on and off. For example: all views on → easy; TIME SERIES and FIXED GLOBE → medium; FIXED GLOBE only → hard.

In this initial version we only consider a single circular repeat-ground-track orbit at 15 rev / sidereal day, or altitude of 561 km. One orbit shifts the ground track 24° west each pass.
